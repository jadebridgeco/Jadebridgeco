FAQ

Before you start, here's what you should know.

Can you source a product if I only have a photo or video?

Yes. Product photos, videos, descriptions and specifications can be used as a starting point for a sourcing request.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. Jade Bridge is positioned to support international clients who need China-based sourcing and procurement assistance.

Can you procure the products for me?

Procurement arrangements can be discussed based on your product, order volume and the scope of support required.

Do you teach people how to import from China?

Yes. Coaching can cover supplier sourcing, platform use, payments, supplier communication, branding, quality inspection and air/sea shipping.

How do I start a sourcing request?

Use the enquiry button below and send your product details, quantity, target price if available and any reference images or specifications.
